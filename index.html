ABOUT US

Indian Hut is a cozy, family-owned Indian restaurant in the heart of Verden. Our dishes blend traditional recipes with modern flair to give you a truly authentic experience. Whether you're craving classic curries, sizzling tandoori, or mouth-watering biryanis — we’ve got you covered.
